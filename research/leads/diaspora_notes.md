## What was found, and how strong it is / क्या मिला और कितना पुख्ता है

### Organisations worth an outreach call (weak-to-moderate leads, NOT village-confirmed)

1. **RANA — Rajasthan Alliance/Association of North America** (`rana.org`, `ranausa.org`, and a defunct/suspended `ranacanada.ca`) — largest Rajasthani diaspora umbrella body found: ~13,000 members, 61 chapters, 17 countries, HQ in SF Bay Area with chapters in NY, LA, Houston, Chicago. **No public chapter listing names Bikaner or Lunkaransar specifically.** Strength: *moderate as a generic contact point* — this is exactly the kind of body the team could email/call and ask "do any of your members trace their family to the 34 villages acquired for Mahajan Field Firing Range?" It is a real, well-established organisation, but public web content gives no direct village tie, so it is a **cold-outreach candidate, not a confirmed lead**.
2. **Rajasthan Foundation (NRR/NRI Department, Govt. of Rajasthan)** (`foundation.rajasthan.gov.in`) — state government's official diaspora-engagement body; just launched the **first Pravasi Rajasthani Divas** (10 December annually) and announced a **Pravasi Rajasthani Meet in Hyderabad on 26 September 2026** (scheduled; not yet held when this was written on 25 September) (per the Rajasthan govt's official DIPR X/Twitter account). This is a live, current (2026) government initiative aimed at cataloguing/honouring Rajasthanis abroad — **worth writing to directly**, since it may hold or be building exactly the kind of diaspora registry the team wants, even though no Bikaner/Lunkaransar-specific detail surfaced in search.
3. **Bikaner Vyapar Udyog Mandal (Bikaner Trade & Industry Board)** — a *local* Bikaner-city body that has itself begun honouring "प्रवासी विशिष्ट हस्तियां" (distinguished expatriates) from Bikaner district, described only as "देश-विदेश में निवासरत" (residing in India and abroad), no countries or villages named in the article found. Strength: *weak-but-locally-relevant* — being Bikaner-based (not just Jaipur-based), it is the most geographically proximate organisation found and could plausibly have honourees or members from the Lunkaransar/Khajuwala/Mahajan belt; worth a direct enquiry.

### News/government content (context only, explicitly NOT diaspora-specific)

4. Patrika's main feature on the displacement ("आंखों में उतर आता है आशियाना उजड़ने का दर्द") is the fullest piece of journalism found on the 34-villages story. It names villages (Bhojrasar, Kumbhana, Motlai, Khanisar, Thoiya, Manera, Duder) and says families were resettled **within Rajasthan** (Khajuwala, Dantuor, Pugal, Mohangadh, Nachna) — it explicitly does **not** mention any family going abroad. Two other Patrika pieces on the firing range ("महाजन के धोरों में उतर चुकी है अमेरिका, ब्रिटेन, रूस और फ्रांस की सेना") are about **foreign armies training at the range**, not diaspora — a recurring false-positive pattern in search results (queries combining "Mahajan" + country names mostly surface Yudh Abhyas joint-exercise news, not migration news).
5. Rajasthan-wide NRI general pieces (e.g. Patrika's Pitru Paksha online pind-daan story quoting "राजस्थान से बड़ी संख्या में लोग अमरीका, कनाडा, ब्रिटेन, दुबई और ऑस्ट्रेलिया में बस चुके हैं") confirm the general pattern of Rajasthani emigration exists and is reported on, but never drill down to Bikaner/Lunkaransar/the 34 villages specifically.

खोज में एक व्यक्ति से जुड़ी अपराध-संबंधी खबरें भी आईं जिनमें लूणकरणसर का ज़िक्र था। यह सांस्कृतिक स्मृति के काम से असंबंधित है और व्यक्ति ने खुद कोई संबंध नहीं बताया, इसलिए इसे सूची में नहीं लिया और यहां नाम या विवरण भी दर्ज नहीं किया। / Crime-news results mentioning Lunkaransar about one individual were excluded as off-purpose; no identifying details are kept.

## Searches that returned genuinely nothing / जिनसे कुछ नहीं मिला

- Every direct **village-name + abroad-term** combination tried (Kumbhana, Berawala, Bhojrasar, Duder, Manera, Khanisar, Motlai, Thoiya + Canada/Dubai/Australia/NRI, in Hindi and English, plain and quoted) returned zero relevant hits — either generic geography/census pages, same-named villages elsewhere in India, or completely unrelated content. This matches the pattern already documented in `social_web_notes.md` for the same village names.
- **Resettlement-town + abroad** searches (Khajuwala, Pugal, Chhatargarh, Bajju, Kolayat, Sherpura, Ranjeetpura + NRI/Canada/Dubai/Gulf) returned nothing village-specific — only generic town/census pages or unrelated NRI-services content (banks, visa consultants).
- **Clan/gotra + abroad** searches (Godara, Dhatarwal, Beniwal, Jakhar, Siyag/Sihag, Saharan, Suthar, Meghwal, Bishnoi, Rathore, Nayak + Canada/Gulf/Bikaner/Lunkaransar) surfaced good **origin-village genealogy** (e.g. confirmed: Khiyana is the traditional origin village of the Dhatarwal Jat gotra; Kumbhana, Kolana, Ajeetmana, Ladera, Manera, Thoiya, Nathor were founded by Dhatarwal descendants; Shekhsar was the old Godara Jat capital) but **nothing about members of these clans living abroad**, confirmed or self-stated.
- **site:linkedin.com** searches for Lunkaransar/Khajuwala + country names returned only unrelated companies (Bikanervala UAE franchise discussion, Bikano Foods) and people with coincidentally similar surnames — no genuine profiles listing one of the 34 villages as hometown.
- YouTube/Instagram vlogger searches for Lunkaransar/Khajuwala/Pugal + Dubai/Canada found only unrelated travel-vlog and Dubai-life-vlog content with no connection to these specific places.
- Academic/remittance-study searches (Gulf migration + Bikaner/Rajasthan/Jat community) returned only studies on Kerala, Bihar, Punjab and Maharashtra migration — **no published study specifically on Gulf/Canada/Australia migration from Bikaner district or the Lunkaransar tehsil belt was found.**
- A search for a dedicated "34 गांव विकास समिति" (34-village welfare/development committee) or Facebook group found nothing — no evidence of an organised alumni/heritage association for the displaced villages exists online yet, abroad-facing or otherwise.

## Assessment: is this findable online, or does it need word of mouth? / आकलन

**Honest answer: this is currently NOT well-findable through public web/social search, and the team should plan on word-of-mouth (WhatsApp, family networks, in-person outreach at melas/fairs, and direct calls to the organisations above) rather than expecting search engines to surface it.**

Reasons:
1. The 34 villages have very short, common Rajasthani place names (Duder, Manera, Thoiya, Rampura, Dewasar, etc.) that collide constantly with same-named villages and generic content elsewhere in India, burying any real signal — the same problem documented in the team's earlier `social_web_notes.md` pass.
2. Facebook/Instagram/YouTube content from small personal accounts — exactly what a displaced-family diaspora post would look like — is poorly indexed by general web search, especially in Hindi/Bagri.
3. No dedicated "34 Gaon" or "Mahajan Firing Range displaced families" association, alumni network, or Facebook/WhatsApp group with any public footprint was found — this kind of memory network, if it exists at all among the diaspora, is almost certainly private (WhatsApp groups, family circles) rather than public content.
4. Existing *general* Rajasthani-diaspora infrastructure is real and reachable (RANA in North America, Rajasthan Foundation's new Pravasi Rajasthani Divas, Bikaner Vyapar Udyog Mandal locally) — these are legitimate, sizeable, and currently active (2026) bodies. **The realistic path is not "search finds a village-specific post" but "the team writes to/calls these three organisations and asks them to help locate members with roots in the 34 villages,"** since their own membership records (private) will know things public search cannot show.
5. Given that even a national/international news-making figure's village link (Lunkaransar) surfaces heavily in search, it strongly suggests that *ordinary* families abroad — who have not made news — simply do not have indexed public content tying them to these specific villages. Their existence abroad is plausible (general Rajasthani/Bikaneri emigration to the Gulf, Canada, Australia, USA, UK is well documented at the state level) but not verifiable person-by-person through this method.

### Recommended next steps for the team
- Contact RANA (rana.org / ranausa.org), Rajasthan Foundation (foundation.rajasthan.gov.in), and Bikaner Vyapar Udyog Mandal directly; ask specifically about members/honourees with roots in Lunkaransar tehsil / the Mahajan Field Firing Range villages.
- Consider registering the "34 Gaon" project itself with a public Facebook page/group, since no such page currently exists online — this would let diaspora members self-identify going forward rather than relying on search to find them retroactively.
- For anyone who does self-identify in the future (per the brief's rule), always require they state the village link themselves, publicly, before recording their name.
