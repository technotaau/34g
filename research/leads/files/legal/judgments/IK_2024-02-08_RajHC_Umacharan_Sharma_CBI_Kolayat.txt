      It is settled law that at the time of framing charges, the Court

is not required to minutely scrutinize the material available on

record. It is enough to frame the charges, if prima facie some

material is available on record to suggest commission of crime

against the accused. In the present matter, F.I.R. was registered on

26.08.2014 at Police Station Gajner, District Bikaner on the basis

of complaint submitted by Gajendra Singh, Tehsildar Kolayat,

District Bikaner. It was alleged in the complaint that one Shri

Bheraram S/o Shri Kishnaram, R/o Rampura, District Bikaner

spared with other public servants with the object to usurp the

government land on the basis of forged and fabricated document

got allotted the land measuring 16.44 hectares in village Goyalri,

Tehsil Kolayat, District Bikaner, Rajasthan and caused wrongful loss

to the Government.
On completion of investigation, initially challan was filed by

the Rajasthan Police against the present petitioners and other co-

accused on 04.08.2015 under
Sections 420
,
467
,
468
,
471
and
120-B
of I.P.C. Later on the investigation was transferred to C.B.I.

for further investigating the matter.
On the application for Enforcement Directorate, the Court of

learned Judicial Magistrate, Kolayat, District Bikaner vide order

dated 16.04.2019 committed the case to the Court of learned

Special Judge, PMLA at Jaipur.
It is also revealed from the investigation that land of 33

villages of Loonkaransar Tehsil, Bikaner was acquired by the

Government of Rajasthan in the year 1983-84 for the purpose of

making Mahajan Field Firing Range for Indian Army.

      The displaced persons were given cash compensation in lieu

of acquired land. The Government also offered to allot land equally

to the acquired land in other Tehsils of District Bikaner to such

displaced persons on payment of nominal amount.
The investigation also reveals that no allotment order was

issued in the name of Bheraram S/o Kishnaram by the Office of

Commissioner Colonization.
The investigation conducted by the C.B.I. also reveals that on

the instruction of accused-petitioners Umacharan, Mahaveer

Swami and other co-accused, the then L.D.C. made entries at

S. Nos.552, 553 and 554 dated 27.11.2006 to the dispatch register

of Revenue Section, Tehsil Kolayat showing dispatch of orders for

mutation which were issued by Shri Fakir Mohd., the then Nayab

Tehsildar. On the basis of order for mutation No.554 dated

27.11.2006 issued by Shri Fakir Mohd., the then Nayab Tehsildar,

petitioners Umacharan and Mahaveer Swami without verifying the

allotment order dated 16.09.1992 made the entries in the name of

accused Bheraram.
The investigation also reveals that the present petitioners in

conspiracy with other private persons carried out mutation of the

land in question in revenue records without following the

procedure laid down in Rajasthan Land Record Rules, 1957.
The     C.B.I.   after       thorough         re-investigation   collected

incriminating material regarding involvement of the accused-

petitioners and filed supplementary charge sheet. As already

stated that at the time of framing charges, the Court is not

required to minutely scrutinize the evidences collected by the

Investigating Agency. The learned trial court vide impugned order

                                   the matter of
Shambhoo Nath Misra Vs. State of U.P. & Ors.
: AIR

                                   1997 SC 2012, held as under:-
"5. The question is : when the public servant is
                                         alleged to have committed the offence of fabrication
                                         of record or misappropriation of public fund etc. can
                                         be said to have acted in discharge of his official
                                         duties? It is not the official duty of the public servant
                                         to fabricate the false record and misappropriate the
                                         public funds etc. in furtherance of or in the discharge
                                         of his official duties. The official capacity only
                                         enables       him    to   fabricate    the   record    or
                                         misappropriate the public fund etc. It does not mean
                                         that it is integrally connected or inseparably
                                         interlinked with the crime committed in the course of
                                         same transaction, as was believed by the learned
                                         judge. Under these circumstances, we are of the
                                         opinion that the view expressed by the High Court as
                                         well as by the trial Court on the question of sanction
                                         is clearly illegal and cannot be sustained."
Therefore, I do not find any illegality or infirmity in the

                                   aforesaid impugned order.
Accordingly, the present criminal revision petition is devoid of

                                   any merit, hence dismissed.
