                    JUSTICE DINESH MEHTA

                                    Order

16/07/2019

     Mr. Bhandari, learned counsel for the                  petitioner informing
the facts appertain to the present writ petition states that by way

of order dated 26.7.2000 passed by the State Government,

alternate land was allotted to the petitioner, in place of her land

situated near Mahajan Field Firing Range, upon payment of

Rs.1,21,480/-. Subsequent thereto, an additional demand was

raised by notice dated 4.7.2011, which was deposited by the

petitioner on 30.12.2011.
The petitioner thereafter, came to receive notice dated

28.4.2015 seeking petitioner's response.                 Though the petitioner

filed a reply dated 19.4.2016, yet the impugned order dated
(Downloaded on 01/09/2019 at 10:14:27 PM)
(2 of 2)                   [CW-9597/2019]



                                   30.5.2019 has been passed cancelling petitioner's allotment on

                                   two counts; (i) the amount deposited by the petitioner was less

                                   and a sum of Rs.18,29,020/- was required to be paid and, (ii) the

                                   petitioner has not surrendered her earlier land.
Mr. Bhandari contends that the amount as determined by the

                                   State     Government    had      been       deposited        by    the   petitioner,

                                   whereafter     additional    amount        too       has    been    deposited    as

                                   demanded by the State Government.                          He argues that the

                                   impugned order dated 30.5.2019 cancelling the allotment for the

                                   differential amount cannot sustain the test of reasonableness. He

                                   further asserts that the petitioner has already surrendered her

                                   Khatedari land near Mahajan Field Firing Range and the same is

                                   not in her possession and it is simply because the requisite entry

                                   in the revenue record has not been made by the revenue

                                   authorities, the petitioner's allotment has been set at naught.
The matter requires consideration.
Admit. Issue notice.       Issue notice of the stay application

                                   also.
Meanwhile, effect and operation of the impugned order dated

                                   30.5.2019 shall remain stayed.
