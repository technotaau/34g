           HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE SANDEEP MEHTA
Order
                                                                         (2 of 2)           [CW-6861/2018]

                                   17/07/2018

                                               By way of this writ petition, the petitioners have

                                   approached this court for assailing the order dated 03.04.2018

                                   passed by the Board of Revenue in Revision No.8458/2007,

                                   whereby the application preferred by the private respondents

                                   under
Order 1 Rule 10 CPC
was allowed and they were permitted

                                   to be impleaded as parties in the revision.
Having heard and considered the arguments advanced

                                   by Mr. Jogendra Singh, learned counsel representing the petitioner

                                   and after going through the impugned order as well as the

                                   contents of the application filed under
Order 1 Rule 10 CPC
filed

                                   by the private respondents herein before the Board of Revenue,

                                   this court is of the firm opinion that the discretion exercised by the

                                   Board of Revenue while allowing the application filed by the

                                   respondents under
Order 1 Rule 10 CPC
and permitting their

                                   impleadment as party respondents in the proceedings by the

                                   impugned order does not suffer from any illegality, irregularity,

                                   perversity or error apparent on the face of record warranting

                                   interference   in   exercise    of   extraordinary/supervisory    writ

                                   jurisdiction of this court.   Otherwise also, no prejudice would be

                                   caused to the petitioner, if the private respondents are heard in

                                   the matter. Hence, the writ petition as well as the stay petition

                                   are dismissed as being devoid of merit.
(SANDEEP MEHTA),J
