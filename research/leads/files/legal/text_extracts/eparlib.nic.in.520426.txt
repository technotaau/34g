GOVERNMENT OF INDIA 
DEFENCE 
LOK SABHA 
UNSTARRED QUESTION NO:394 
ANSWERED ON:0.2.2005 


INDO-RUSSIAN JOINT DEFENCE EXERCISE 
Khanna Shri Avinash Rai;Mahajan Shri Y.G.;Ponnuswamy Shri E. 


Will the Minister of DEFENCE be pleased to state: 


(a) whether the defence forces of India and Russia conducted a joint exercise to counter terrorism; 

(b) if so, the details thereof; 

(c) whether a great loss was averted during an accident that occurred during Indo-Russia joint defence exercise; 
(d) if so, the details thereof; 

(e) whether any inquiry has been conducted in this regard; 

(f) if so, the details of recommendations made in the inquiry report; and 


(g) the action taken by the Government in this regard? 


Answer 


MINISTER OF DEFENCE (SHRI PRANAB MUKHERJEE) 


(a) to (g): A statement is attached. 


STATEMENT REFERRED TO IN REPLY TO PARTS (a) TO (७) OF LOK SABHA UNSTARRED QUESTION NO. 39i FOR 


4.42.2005 


The first ever Indo-Russian Airborne exercise INDRA-06 was held at Agra and Mahajan Field Firing Range from 0-9 October 2005. 
The joint exercise was based on the theme of Counter Terrorist Operations under a United Nations mandate. Indian para troopers and 


Russian airborne personnel participated in the exercise. 


2. The Russian Defence Minister Mr. S.B. lvanov witnessed the exercise on 76th October 2005. During the course of para-drops, one 
of the loads dropped from an aircraft landed in the proximity to the grand stand put up for the spectators. This was due to strong upper 


level winds. No accident occurred during the exercise.
