2.2 Established Defence Presence 


Rajasthan's established defence presence includes strategic military installations and 
research facilities, significantly bolstering its defence readiness and operational 
capabilities.

The Southwestern Command, headquartered in Jaipur, is a pivotal operational 
command of the Indian Army responsible for border security and rapid 
deployment of forces. This command manages critical tactical operations and 
maintains a state of heightened preparedness due to its proximity to the 
international border. 


The Defence Research and Development Organisation’s (DRDO) Defence 
Lab in Jodhpur specialises in crucial strategic research, including advanced 
material sciences, camouflage technologies, and life estimation of weapons 
systems under harsh desert conditions. This facility not only supports national 
defence objectives but also facilitates innovative research and technological 
advancements essential for modern warfare. 


Further strengthening Rajasthan’s defence capabilities is the Mahajan Field 
Firing Range (MFFR), India’s largest desert training facility. The MFFR 
offers extensive terrain ideal for comprehensive training exercises, simulating 
realistic battlefield conditions. The vastness and harshness of this training area 
make it an unparalleled location for military exercises and training programs, 
enhancing combat readiness and tactical proficiency of the armed forces. 


2.3 Robust Supporting Industries 


2.4 


Rajasthan’s robust industrial ecosystem provides critical support to the Aerospace 
and Defence manufacturing sectors, leveraging its existing industrial strengths. 


The state's Metal Machining Sector, which includes specialised steel, copper, 
and brass units, is crucial for manufacturing ammunition and other defence 
components. These industries provide essential raw materials and components, 
ensuring seamless integration into the A&D manufacturing supply chain. 


The well-established Automotive Industry Ecosystem in Rajasthan further 
enhances the state's attractiveness. Over 100 companies operate across various 
segments such as engines, transmissions, electronic components, and 
automotive parts. These industries offer substantial synergies for defence 
equipment manufacturing, including mechanical and electronic gadgetry, 
critical for various defence applications. 


The state also hosts a significant Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul (MRO) 
facility operated by Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL) in Jodhpur. This facility 
provides strategic equipment maintenance, tool-room capabilities, and 
specialised research and development, all vital for sustained operational 
readiness and long-term equipment reliability in defence applications. 


Flexible Land Availability 


Rajasthan offers abundant land resources that are strategically positioned and highly 
suitable for aerospace and defence manufacturing and testing operations. The state 
has substantial government-owned land parcels, as well as privately held land, 
available for development into manufacturing units, testing sites, and research 
facilities.
