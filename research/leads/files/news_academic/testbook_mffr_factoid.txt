                    Detailed Solution
The correct answer is Rajasthan.
- Mahajan Field Firing Ranges is located in Rajasthan.
Key Points
- Mahajan Field Firing Ranges were acquired in 1982 and are the second-largest Ranges in the country with an area of 3.37 lakh acre of land.
- Annually, the Ranges are utilised by approximately 600 units which translates itself into 2,30,000 troops.
- Mahajan Field Firing Ranges have also been designated as the integrated training hub for the conduct of joint exercises with friendly foreign and partner countries which is on the increase as India extends its soft power to South-East Asian, African and Central Asian Republics.
Additional Information
- Field Firing Ranges in Rajasthan:
	
  - Balwanta (Nasirabad)
  - Mahajan
  - Pokhran
