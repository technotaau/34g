TITLE: Rajasthan land scam: Six people arrested
DATE: 2015-07-22
AUTHOR: Authors; Contributors
RETRIEVED: 2026-09-25

Six persons have been arrested so far in connection with controversial land deals, one of which was related to sale of plot belonging to Robert Vadra’s company, in Kolayat and Gajner area of the State, the police said on Tuesday.
Jaiprakash was arrested on Monday while five others were arrested over the last few days for wrongfully transferring government land by preparing fake documents in Kolayat and Gajner area, Deputy SP Kolayat, Ramavtar Soni said.
Umacharan, a patwari and already suspended in the case, Fakir Mohammad, Ranjit Singh, Kishore Singh and Gugan are the other accused who were arrested earlier.
Around 1,372 bigha government land was sold to different companies and private people in 2006, with the help of fake documents prepared by private men in connivance with the revenue department, he said.
Nearly 275 bigha land was sold to Robert Vadra’s company, Mr. Soni said.
The police said that 18 FIRs have been registered in the scam so far.
The land was illegally allotted to villagers in 2006-7 who were “wrongfully” shown in revenue records as farmers displaced by Mahajan Field Firing Range of the Army, Mr. Soni said.
The government had acquired land of 34 villages for the firing range in 1982-83 and allotted lands to displaced people in other parts of the district, including Kolayat and Gajner, between 1992 and 1996, he said.
The accused allegedly got allotted/transferred lands in the name of displaced farmers and sold them later and Vadra’s company also allegedly purchased some of those lands. The matter came to light during the previous Congress government and an inquiry in the matter was ordered when BJP government led by Vasundhara Raje came to power in 2013. - PTI
