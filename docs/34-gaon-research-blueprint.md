# 34 Gaon of Mahajan: Research Blueprint for a Cultural Preservation Website

**Prepared by:** TechnoTaau Team (lead: Jakhar Singh)
**Date:** 4 September 2026
**Scope:** All online-available, verifiable information on the 33/34 villages of Lunkaransar tehsil (Mahajan sub-tehsil), Bikaner, Rajasthan, that were acquired for the Mahajan Field Firing Range (MFFR), organised into five pillars for website design.

> **How to read this document.** Every fact carries a status tag:
> **[Confirmed]** = primary or reputable source fetched and read.
> **[Press]** = reported by Rajasthan Patrika, Deccan Herald, Tribune etc., not cross-checked against an official record.
> **[Community]** = community-written history (Hindi Wikipedia user page / Jatland), valuable but unofficial.
> **[Unverified]** = a claim seen only in a search snippet, a social post, or inferred by us.
> Nothing in this document is invented. Where the internet is silent, we say so and name the offline archive to visit next.

## Executive summary

- **The number is 33 by official count, 34 by local memory.** The Ministry of Defence told the Lok Sabha on 21 August 1987 that **33 villages** were shifted. The Rajasthan High Court (2024) repeats "33 villages of Loonkaransar Tehsil". Rajasthan Patrika consistently says **34 गांव**. The website should honour the local name "34 Gaon" while stating the official figure transparently. [Confirmed]
- **The range is enormous and the villages have legally ceased to exist.** Census 2011 records the whole acquired tract as one "village", *Mahajan Fieldfiring Range* (code 069202), area **136,406 ha** (about 3.37 lakh acres), population 2. The old villages have no census codes of their own any more. [Confirmed]
- **Only 15 of the 33/34 names have been recovered online.** Seven from Patrika, two from the 1987 Lok Sabha answer, and nine (three overlapping) from the Dhatarwal Jat community history. The complete list exists offline: in the Land Acquisition notifications of 1982–84 (Rajasthan Gazette), the 1981 District Census Handbook for Bikaner, and the Colonisation Commissioner's "MFFR Branch" files. [Confirmed gap]
- **The acquisition was multi-phase:** a Rule 20B for MFFR oustees already existed in **1977**; the main acquisition ran **1982–84** (evacuation 1981–86 per oral history); compensation of **Rs 64.11 crore** was paid for private land; an **expansion** followed in **1992–96**. [Confirmed / Press]
- **Rehabilitation moved people 100–200 km west** into the Indira Gandhi Nahar Pariyojana (IGNP) command: Khajuwala, Pugal, Chhattargarh, Dantor, Bajju/Ranjeetpura, Kolayat, and Mohangarh and Nachna in Jaisalmer. Forty years on, **148 allotment cases are still pending** and about **350 families** are stuck because their allotted land overlaps Forest Department claims (Patrika, Feb 2025). [Press / Confirmed via HC orders]
- **Ranjeetpura is not in Lunkaransar.** It is a resettlement-era village in Kolayat/Bajju tehsil (census code 069378, population 5,559), 140 km from Bikaner. High Court petitions confirm MFFR oustee families (Dhatarwal) live at "Post Ranjeetpura, Tehsil Bajju". [Confirmed]
- **The UXO story is real but smaller online than assumed.** Confirmed MFFR-linked civilian deaths from scrap are two (2014, 2019), both contractor labour from outside the district. Live shells keep turning up in fields and canals (2018, 2025, 2026). Soldier deaths at the range are better documented (2019, Dec 2024, Feb 2025). No aggregate casualty figure, NGO report, PIL or NHRC proceeding exists online. [Confirmed]
- **The living heritage is the Holi return.** Every year, hundreds of former residents enter the range on Holi to burn Holika at Kumbhana and visit the lok-devta shrines of their ruined villages. Those ruins are also used by the Army as mock villages in exercises with the USA, UK, France, Russia and Oman. This is the emotional core of the website. [Press]

## Pillar 2: Historical Timeline and Legal Acquisition (1938 to 2026)

### 2.1 Dated timeline

- **1938 [Confirmed]** The Manoeuvres, Field Firing and Artillery Practice Act, 1938, lets a state notify an area for periodic field firing. Rajasthan framed its Rules in 1955. No MFFR-specific notification under this Act was found; the courts describe MFFR as land *acquired* and vested in the Government of India, an owned range rather than a periodically notified one. [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2305/1/AAAA1938___)5.pdf)
- **9 Sept 1977 [Confirmed]** Rule 20B, "Allotment of land to persons/families displaced from Mahajan Field Firing Range, Bikaner", inserted into the Rajasthan Land Revenue (Allotment of Land for Agricultural Purposes) Rules, 1970. Land allotted "shall be equivalent to the land from which such family has been displaced subject to the ceiling limit". This means **displacement for MFFR began before 1982**; the 1982–84 acquisition was an enlargement. [indiankanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/86733848/)
- **1981 [Confirmed]** File F.3(8)/Reve./Col./81 opened for MFFR rehabilitation (file number cited in the 1985 notification). Oral history dates the first evacuations to 1981. [Community]
- **1982 [Confirmed]** "Land was acquired in the year 1982 for Mahajan Field Firing Range of Indian Army; the award was passed and the land was entered in the name of Government of India in the record of rights." Rajasthan HC, *Ramesh Chandra v. Chandra Singh*, 24 Jan 2017. [indiankanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/174753246/). The Army's own description: "acquired in 1982 ... second-largest Ranges in the country with an area of 3.37 lakh acre". [testbook quoting MoD text](https://testbook.com/question-answer/mahajan-field-firing-ranges-is-located-in-which-st--5a5b7831398be74d8badcf17)
- **1983–84 [Confirmed]** "Land of 33 villages of Loonkaransar Tehsil, Bikaner was acquired by the Government of Rajasthan in the year 1983-84 ... The displaced persons were given cash compensation in lieu of acquired land. The Government also offered to allot land equally to the acquired land in other Tehsils of District Bikaner ... on payment of nominal amount." *Umacharan Sharma v. State*, 8 Feb 2024. [indiankanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/26436710/)
- **1984–85 [Press]** Patrika's date for the acquisition: "रक्षा मंत्रालय ने वर्ष 1984-85 में महाजन क्षेत्र के 34 गांवों की भूमि को अधिग्रहित किया था।" [Patrika, 5 Nov 2018](https://www.patrika.com/bikaner-news/mahajan-firing-range-3673091)
- **23 Nov 1985 [Confirmed]** Notification F.3(8) GSR.162/Reve./Col./81 (Gazette Extraordinary, 10 Jan 1986): oustees offered barani land anywhere in Bikaner district at **Rs 300 per bigha**, or IGNP command/uncommand land at twice the reserve price; **2 bighas barani = 1 bigha command**; area equal to that lost, subject to ceiling; powers to the Colonisation Commissioner under Rule 24 of the Rajasthan Colonisation (IGNP Colony Area) Rules, 1975. *State v. Hajari Ram*, AIR 1997 Raj 71. [indiankanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/38464/)
- **1986–87 [Confirmed]** First allotments: Chak 5 SDWMU (19 Jan 1987), Chak 3 KPM Chhatargarh (5 Sep 1987), Chak 1 RSM; canal water reaches some chaks via Rajmalsar/Jaimalsar distributaries in 1987. Typical case: 40 bighas for Rs 12,000.
- **21 Aug 1987 [Confirmed]** Lok Sabha answer: 33 villages, Rs 64.11 crore compensation, Rs 2.73 crore undisbursed (see Pillar 1).
- **1987–1997 [Confirmed]** State tries to re-price allotments (extra demands of Rs 31,500; circular of 19 Oct 1987 charging command rates). HC quashes them in 1997 (*Hajari Ram*, 22 appeals): promissory estoppel protects the Rs 300/bigha promise.
- **1991–1993 [Confirmed]** Forest Department claims oustee allotments in Chhatargarh, Khajuwala and Pugal (cancellation notices 19 Jul 1991, cancellations 4 Aug 1993). *Rup Ram v. State* (CWP 3807/1992) allowed 27 May 1993, affirmed 11 May 2000, Forest SLP dismissed by the Supreme Court 26 Mar 2001. [recited in *Ram Singh*, 2025](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/150464229/)
- **1992–1996 [Press]** "Land was acquired in Bikaner, between 1992 to 1996, for expansion of Mahajan Field Firing Range of Indian Army. A number of people were displaced and they were promised compensation and allotment of land in other places." [Deccan Herald, 4 Jan 2015](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/18-fake-mutations-agro-land-2105198)
- **24 Mar 1999 [Confirmed]** *State v. Subhash Ram*: oustees promised land in 1986 were first given Bhoodan land (cancelled), then left waiting a decade; HC orders allotment at the 1986 rate. [indiankanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/77233/)
- **1998–2001 [Confirmed]** Further allotment notifications (28 Aug 1998, 4 Oct 1999, 29 Mar 2001); allotments at Bajju village in 2001. [*Bachna Ram*, 2025](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/194551084/)
- **2005–2006 [Press]** Fake mutations opened in revenue records in the names of MFFR displaced persons at Goyalri, Gajner, Indo ka Bala and Mand (Kolayat); land later sold to private companies.
- **11 Sept 2007 [Press / Confirmed]** Revenue Department authorises the Additional Commissioner, Colonisation, Bikaner, to allot replacement land to MFFR oustees; Rule 20B re-notified (GSR 54, 6 Sep 2007).
- **Sept 2014 [Press]** Collector Arti Dogra cancels 18 fake mutations (about 360 ha). MP Arjun Ram Meghwal alleges 40% was bought by Skylight Hospitality. "It's an old issue related to Mahajan Field Firing Range." [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/18-fake-mutations-agro-land-2105198)
- **10 Oct 2014 [Confirmed]** State decides to treat the Rup Ram / Teeja Devi judgments as judgments in rem for all similarly placed MFFR oustees. [*Ram Singh*, 2025]
- **2016–2020 [Confirmed]** Continuing litigation: *Heera Ram* (2016, cancellation after 29 years' cultivation set aside), *Saroj Gupta* (2019, Rs 18.29 lakh extra demand stayed), *Buddhu Khan* (2020, Deharia allotments). [indiankanoon search, 65 results](https://indiankanoon.org/search/?formInput=%22Mahajan+Field+Firing+Range%22)
- **8 Feb 2024 [Confirmed]** *Umacharan Sharma*: HC refuses to quash forgery charges against revenue officials for a fake MFFR-oustee allotment (16.44 ha, Goyalri).
- **17 Jan 2025 [Press]** Revenue Department (Deputy Secretary Birdhichand Gangwal) **withdraws the Colonisation Department's 2007 allotment powers**; files move to the Collector / headquarters. **148 allotment cases pending; about 350 farmers** stuck in Forest Department overlap. Headline: "देश के लिए भूमि देकर 40 साल से भटक रहे किसान, नहीं मिली जमीन". [Patrika, 14 Feb 2025](https://www.patrika.com/bikaner-news/farmers-have-been-wandering-for-40-years-after-giving-their-land-for-the-country-19397378)
- **19 Feb 2025 [Confirmed]** Rajasthan HC (Justice Vinit Kumar Mathur) disposes of a batch of about 45 oustee writs (Ram Singh, Sharif Khan, Ravi Mirdha, Gomati Devi, Jagdish Prasad Godara, Bhalu Khan and others) directing the Assistant Commissioners, Colonisation (Chhatargarh, Khajuwala, Pugal) to decide pending khatedari applications within **eight weeks**. [indiankanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/150464229/)
- **12 Mar 2025 [Press]** ACB FIR against 15 revenue officials and 31 beneficiaries for fake allotment of 508.68 ha at Pugal; loss about Rs 40 crore. [Patrika](https://www.patrika.com/bikaner-news/fake-allotment-case-of-2-thousand-bigha-land-fir-registered-in-acb-against-sdm-including-15-employees-19456061)
- **25 Mar 2025 [Press]** 1,400 bighas reserved for ex-servicemen, "महाजन फायरिंग रेंज के विस्थापितों" and the landless fraudulently allotted at Kolayat and Bajju (69 cases, 2020–23) and sold to solar companies; Revenue Minister Hemant Meena acknowledges it in the Assembly. [Patrika](https://www.patrika.com/bikaner-news/fake-allotment-of-1400-bigha-land-in-kolayat-and-bajju-19483920)
- **7 May 2025 [Confirmed]** *Daleep Kumar v. State*, co-petitioner the **Mahajan Field Firing Range Visthapit Vikas Samiti, Khajuwala** (secretary Brij Lal), dismissed for non-prosecution. This confirms an organised oustees' body exists. [indiankanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/48956184/)
- **30 Apr 2026 [Press]** Patrika: hundreds of rounds fired daily; blasts heard 25–30 km away. [Patrika](https://www.patrika.com/bikaner-news/mahajan-field-firing-range-in-rajasthan-hundreds-of-bombs-fired-every-day-20541582)

### 2.3 Recent boundary, survey and geopolitical context

- **Nationwide defence-land survey [Confirmed]:** the Ministry of Defence surveyed 17.78 lakh acres of defence land between Oct 2018 and Jan 2022 using Electronic Total Station, **DGPS, drones and satellite imagery** with state revenue departments. Rajasthan was the first state where drone imagery was used, "completing in weeks what took years". MFFR is not named but, as MoD-owned land, falls inside the survey. [ThePrint Hindi](https://hindi.theprint.in/defence/defence-ministry-completes-quite-large-digital-survey-of-17-78-lakh-acres-of-land-why-how-and-what-next/262195/)
- **Not found online after about 400 searches:** any DGPS/drone survey report specific to MFFR, pillar (पिलर) demarcation disputes, an Army-versus-Revenue boundary conflict, encroachment cases on range land, range expansion proposals, NOC or construction bans in surrounding villages, or Assembly questions by Sumit Godara, Manik Chand Surana or Virendra Beniwal on MFFR. Dainik Bhaskar could not be crawled. Treat all such claims as **[Unverified]** until a Bhaskar clipping or RTI reply is obtained.
- **The live dispute is not about the range boundary.** It is oustee-versus-State/Forest Department over replacement land 100–200 km away in the IGNP command, plus the repeated theft of land reserved for oustees by fraud.
- **A warning about a common data error:** the dates "Section 4 notification 16.11.1982, award 19.09.1983, possession 26.03.1983" that circulate online belong to *Dhan Devi v. Union of India* (Punjab and Haryana HC, 2022), a different field firing range. Do not use them for MFFR. [indiankanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/106639542/)

### 2.4 Military use of the range [Confirmed unless marked]

- Second-largest range in India after Pokhran; about 600 units and 2,30,000 troops train annually; designated "integrated training hub" for joint exercises with foreign partners (MoD text quoted on testbook and OSM).
- Named camps: North Camp ("Charlie Centre"), Eastern Camp; scrap store on the Sri Ganganagar–Bikaner highway. Runs under South Western Command; Suratgarh and Bikaner military stations are the nearest. **No "Ranjeetpura Military Station" exists.**
- Foreign exercises at MFFR: Indra 2013 and 2015 (Russia); Shakti 2016 and 2019 (France); Ajeya Warrior 2017 and Nov 2025 (UK); Yudh Abhyas Feb 2021 and **Sept 2024** (USA, first HIMARS demonstration, [DD News](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/indian-and-american-armies-begin-joint-military-exercise-at-mahajan-field-firing-range-in-bikaner)); Al Najah 2022 (Oman); Austra Hind 2022 (Australia); Cyclone Feb 2025 (Egypt); Harimau Shakti 2025 (Malaysia); Dharma Guardian (Japan). [Patrika, 2 Aug 2022](https://www.patrika.com/bikaner-news/mahajan-field-firing-range-7687974)
- Indian exercises: Vijay Prahar (Apr–May 2018, 20,000+ troops, [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1530866)); Kharga Shakti Nov 2024 (swarm drones, loiter munitions) and Feb 2026 (Bhairav commando battalions, counter-UAS, [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/defence/exercise-kharga-shakti-2026-army-validates-swift-transition-from-deterrence-to-decisive-action/)); K9 Vajra drills. Shatrujeet 2016 at MFFR is widely repeated but **[Unverified]**; Sarvatra Prahar 2016 was at Devlali, not Mahajan.
- The ruins of the 34 villages are used as mock settlements: "गांवों में बने मकान, तालाब, मंदिर आदि आज भी यहां पर मौजूद हैं" and serve as targets in exercises with the USA, Russia and France. [Patrika 2022]

### 3.2 Folk deities and sacred geography

- **Inside the range [Press]:** "प्राचीन मंदिर व लोक देवताओं के थान" survive in the ruins; fairs are still held on specific tithis; Holika dahan at Kumbhana. A **devli of Nathu Dada near Khiyana** is claimed inside the range [Unverified snippet].
- **Regional pantheon [Confirmed, Gazetteer]:** Bhairon ji, Hanuman ji, Shitla, **Pabuji, Ramdevji, Gogaji**, Hinglaj Mata, Mawarian, **Karni ji**, Babhuta Sidh, Kesaria Kanwar; worship of the **khejri** tree; Bhopa/Bhopi serve the shrines.
- **Gogaji (Jahar Veer):** Chauhan hero-saint of Dadrewa (Churu), protector against snakebite, honoured by Hindus and Muslims alike; Gogamedi fair on Bhadrapada Krishna 9; pilgrims walk with blue nishan flags. Rao Lunkaran conquered Dadrewa in 1509. [Wikipedia Gogaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gogaji)
- **Ramdevji fairs in the tehsil:** Kenyara (Lunkaransar), Magh Sudi 10. [Gazetteer]
- **Karni Mata (Deshnok):** blessed Rao Bika's founding of Bikaner (1485–88). [Wikipedia Rao Bika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rao_Bika)
- **Kalu:** Kalaka Mata temple, village about 200 years old, Nahata haveli.
- **Jasnath / Sidh Jat fire dance** at Katariyasar.
- **Lunkaransar town:** named after Rao Lunkaran (r. 1505–26, "कलियुग का कर्ण"), and the salt (lun) playa lake. [hi.wikipedia](https://hi.wikipedia.org/wiki/लूनकरनसर)

### 3.3 Traditional economy of the drylands [Confirmed, Gazetteer and breed literature]

- **Cropping:** rainfed bajra, moth, moong, guar; famine foods sangri (khejri pod), ker, phog, bhurat grass seed. Dishes: rabodi/rabdi, khichra (Akha Teej speciality), churma, dal-ka-sira.
- **Pastoralism:** the "Rathi tract" of Lunkaransar; Rathi cows (about 1,530 kg per lactation), **Magra sheep** (the only lustrous carpet-wool breed, now declining), Bikaneri camels; grasses sewan, dhaman, ganthil. Sheep and wool extension centres opened at Lunkaransar (1954–55) and Mahajan (1962–63); Mahajan cattle fair listed in 1958.
- **Water:** taanka (underground cistern), johad and kuan; the Dhatarwal founding story is literally a story of digging johads and wells.
- **Dwellings:** circular mud-and-thatch **jhopras** in clusters of three or four with a **kothi** grain store and a thorn-hedge enclosure. These are exactly the ruins ("थेह बनते पुराने मकान") now visible inside the range.
- **The canal transformation:** the IGNP enters Bikaner district at **Kharbara, Lunkaransar tehsil**; Lunkaransar is now the "Rajkot of Rajasthan" for groundnut, with over 1.9 lakh tonnes of mandi arrivals a year and India's first olive-oil refinery. The oustees were moved from a rainfed world into a canal world in one generation. [Wikipedia Lunkaransar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunkaransar)

### 3.4 Living culture

- **Dialect:** Marwari with Thali and Bagri; 1961 Census returned "Rathi" as a separate mother tongue in the district.
- **Music:** songs to the **chang** (sheepskin frame drum), ragas Mand, Loom, Holi; Pabuji-ke-bhope by Kamadias; Bikaner's Rammat Holi drama.
- **Marriage:** bride-price rather than dowry among Jats; widow remarriage (nata/kareva, "choori pahnana"); strict gotra exogamy.
- **Craft:** Kashida embroidery by Meghwal and Jat women; carpet wool.
- **Fairs:** Gogamedi, Kolayat Kapil Muni (Kartik Purnima), Deshnok, Mukam, Katariyasar, Kenyara, Mahajan cattle fair.
- **Displacement ritual:** the annual Holi return to Kumbhana and the shrine fairs inside the range. The local press phrase is "आशियाना उजड़ने का दर्द".

### 4.4 Daily life on the periphery

- **Sound and vibration:** hundreds of rounds a day; blasts audible 25–30 km away in Baladesar, Gusaina, Manoharia, Ramsara, Phuleji, Chak Jod, Asrasar, Jaswantsar (Patrika, Apr 2026). House-crack and livestock-loss complaints were not found in the fetched text [Unverified].
- **Livestock straying:** confirmed by the 2019 Banwari Lal injury; no source on cattle or camel deaths or compensation for animals.
- **Land and construction restrictions:** one snippet mentions agriculture-only use near the range under the 1938 Act; source page not located [Unverified].
- **Water, roads, schools, health, mobile coverage:** no MFFR-specific reporting; the Census "village" of the range shows density 16/km², 25 km from Mahajan railway station, 73 km from Nal airport.
- **The real chronic crisis is administrative:** 40 years of unresolved land allotment, Forest Department overlap, and fraud against the oustee land pool (Pillars 2.1 and 2.3).
- **Governance and rights bodies:** no NGO report, RTI story, PIL, NHRC or Rajasthan Human Rights Commission proceeding on MFFR civilian deaths exists online. The only organised voice found is the Mahajan Field Firing Range Visthapit Vikas Samiti, Khajuwala.

### 4.5 What this means for the website's framing

- Lead with the **documented** crisis (land, forest overlap, fraud, four decades of litigation, Holi as the only permitted return) and present UXO as a **recurring hazard with a thin public record**, inviting community-submitted incident reports rather than asserting unverified death tolls.
- Build a **community incident form** so villagers can log bomb finds and injuries with photo, date and location; this will create the first aggregate dataset that no newspaper or agency currently holds.

## Pillar 5: Website Architecture Recommendation

### 5.1 Brand and tone

- Working title: **"34 Gaon: Mahajan ri Maati"** (bilingual, Hindi/Rajasthani first, English second).
- Tone: empathetic, documentary, evidence-tagged. Every page shows a confidence badge (Confirmed / Press / Community / Unverified) so the site becomes a trusted archive, not a rumour board.
- Visual language: desert ochre, khejri green, indigo (the Gogaji nishan colour); typography with a Devanagari face (e.g. Mukta or Baloo 2) paired with a humanist Latin face.

### 5.2 Sitemap (multi-page)

1. **Home (घर)**
   - Hero: a slow pan across the ruins with the line "1984 में 34 गांव उठ गए। यादें नहीं उठीं।"
   - Four numbers that matter: 33/34 villages, 1,364 km², Rs 64.11 crore, 40 years pending.
   - "This Holi, we go back" countdown to the annual return.
   - Latest community submissions strip.
2. **The 34 Villages Directory (गांव)**
   - Card grid: one card per village, with status badge, clan, deity, known families, resettlement destinations. 15 cards populated now, 18–19 "help us name this village" placeholder cards that invite submissions.
   - Each village page: map pin (old site inside the range polygon), 1981 population once the DCH is obtained, oral histories, photo gallery, family tree submissions, shrine and fair calendar.
   - Sister-village links (Soolera, Bheekhnera, Rekh Meghana) and resettlement links (Ranjeetpura, Khajuwala, Pugal chaks).
3. **The Map (नक्शा)**
   - Interactive Leaflet/MapLibre map with the OSM range polygon, old village points, resettlement chaks 100–200 km west, and "sound radius" villages. Toggle layers: 1981 villages, 1987 allotments, 2025 pending cases, UXO finds.
4. **Timeline and Law (इतिहास और कानून)**
   - Scroll-driven timeline from 1349 (Nathu Dhatarwal) through 1488 (Bika), 1562 (Mahajan thikana), 1977 (Rule 20B), 1982–84, 1985, 1987, 1992–96, 2007, 2014, 2025, 2026.
   - Document vault: Lok Sabha 1987 answer image, HC judgments, the 1985 notification, Rule 20B text, each with plain-Hindi summary.

   - Audio-first: 3–8 minute recorded testimonies with Hindi transcript and English summary.

   - Folk deities and fairs calendar (Gogamedi, Kenyara, Kumbhana Holi).

   - Incident register with the table above, filterable by year and type.
   - Community incident form (photo, GPS, date, injured/killed, ordnance type) with moderation.

   - Threads by village; WhatsApp-style simplicity; phone-number login; Hindi keyboard support; moderators from the Visthapit Vikas Samiti.

### 5.3 Creative presentation ideas

- **"Then/Now" slider** on each village page: 1980s family photo over today's ruin photo, aligned by hand.
- **Holi Return livestream** each year, geotagged, with a public "light a diya" counter.
- **Sound map:** field recordings of the range at firing time from 5, 15 and 30 km, so visitors hear what peripheral villages hear.
- **Ruins as monuments:** 360° photo spheres of the surviving temples and jhopra clusters shot during permitted fair-day entries.
- **Compensation calculator:** shows what Rs 300 per bigha in 1986 means today and what 40 bighas of IGNP command land is worth now, making the injustice legible.
- **Clan-tree wall:** a zoomable genealogy starting from Nathu Dhatarwal (1349) or Pandu Godara, growing as families submit branches.
- **Document forensics cards:** side-by-side of a genuine 1987 allotment order and a forged 2005 mutation, teaching families how to check their own papers.
- **"Kaun sa gaon?" quiz:** crowdsourcing the 18 missing names by showing old patta fragments and asking elders.
- **Short vertical videos (60–90 s)** in Rajasthani with Hindi and English subtitles for Instagram and YouTube Shorts, since the region's own bomb-find reports already travel as reels.

### 5.4 Technical stack recommendation

- **Front end:** Next.js or Astro, static-first for low-bandwidth rural users; Hindi as default locale, English toggle.
- **Maps:** MapLibre GL with OSM tiles; store the range polygon (OSM way 412765540) and village points as GeoJSON in the repo.
- **Content:** Markdown/MDX in the repo for pages; a headless CMS (Decap or Payload) for community submissions.
- **Media:** Cloudinary or self-hosted MinIO; audio transcripts via Whisper with manual Hindi correction.
- **Forum:** Discourse (Hindi locale) or a lightweight Supabase-backed chaupal with phone OTP.
- **Data model:** `villages.csv` (this repo, `data/`), `incidents.csv`, `resettlement_sites.csv`, `documents.json`, all with a `status` column mirroring the confidence tags.
- **Accessibility:** large type, high contrast, audio versions of every key page for non-literate elders (female literacy in Ranjeetpura is 42.5%).
